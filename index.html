About Us

Established in 1998, McNamara's Pub brings an authentic Irish dive bar experience to the heart of the Cudell neighborhood. Housed in a classic brick building with a welcoming stone-floored patio, the pub is known for its warm atmosphere, rotating taps, and loyal crowd. Whether you’re dropping in for a pint of Guinness or catching up with friends outside, McNamara’s offers a laid-back, genuine neighborhood pub experience where everyone feels like a regular.
